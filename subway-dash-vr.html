Subway Dash VR

Dodge trains, jump barriers, slide under bars.
Keyboard: ←/A ,→/D lanes · Space jump · ↓/S slide · P or Esc pause · R restart · G toggle gestures
VR controllers: stick left/right = lane, stick up or A = jump, stick down or B = slide, trigger = start/resume, Y = pause, X = gestures on/off
VR gestures: lean = lane · raise a hand = jump · crouch = slide (turn off for seated play)
